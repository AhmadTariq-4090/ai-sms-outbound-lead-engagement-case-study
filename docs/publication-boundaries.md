# Publication boundaries

AI SMS Outbound & Lead Engagement System is the anonymized portfolio title for confidential client work. This public repository has its own history and contains newly written case-study documentation only.

Excluded from public publication:

- Client identity, branding, staff names, license identifiers, and production addresses.
- Application source, internal documents, exact message templates, and model prompts.
- Commercial terms, commission structures, and proprietary business positioning.
- Contact lists, phone numbers, email addresses, conversations, and campaign records.
- Session cookies, credentials, service configuration, and machine-specific settings.
- Client screenshots, document scans, and third-party forms.

The narrative explains the general business problem and engineering work. It does not claim measured revenue, conversion, cost, or throughput gains, and it does not certify regulatory compliance. It grants no rights to the confidential implementation.
